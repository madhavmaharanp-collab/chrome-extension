## Detailed Description

**Audio Master — Take Control of Your Sound.**

Audio Master is a professional, desktop-grade audio mastering and sound enhancement extension designed for Google Chrome. Whether you are listening to music, watching movies, attending lectures, or streaming on YouTube, Audio Master gives you total acoustic command over your browser sound.

---

### 🌟 Key Features

#### 🎚️ 10-Band Precision Equalizer
Fine-tune frequencies from deep sub-bass to crystal air:
- 60 Hz (Deep Bass)
- 170 Hz (Bass Punch)
- 310 Hz (Low Midrange)
- 600 Hz (Midrange Body)
- 1 kHz (Vocal Body)
- 3 kHz (Vocal Clarity)
- 6 kHz (Presence)
- 12 kHz (High Treble)
- 14 kHz (Air)
- 16 kHz (Ultra High)

#### 🚀 Real Volume Booster (Up to 200%)
- Amplify low-volume videos and quiet lectures safely up to 200%.
- Integrated **anti-clipping peak limiter** and soft saturation curve to prevent harsh digital distortion.

#### 🎙️ Audio Separation Studio
- **Vocals Only:** Isolate dialogue and vocals while suppressing instrumental background noise.
- **Music Only:** Suppress vocals for instant karaoke and clean instrumental listening.
- **Custom Mix:** Independently balance vocal levels (0–200%) and instrumental music (0–200%).
- Real-time zero-latency DSP phase cancellation with local on-device processing.

#### 🎧 Spotify Mode / Premium Sound Engine
Enhance your favorite web music player with 4 dedicated acoustic profiles:
- **Studio Quality:** Transparent, linear reference response with subtle warmth.
- **Spotify Bass:** Resonant, punchy low-end without mid-bass muddiness.
- **Immersive (3D):** Broadened soundstage and spatial acoustic depth for headphones.
- **Clear Audio:** High-definition vocal presence and crisp percussion.

#### 🌐 Automated Website Profiles
Save custom sound settings for individual sites (YouTube, Spotify, Netflix, Twitch) that load automatically when you open them.

#### 🎛️ Floating Mini Player
Compact, floating pill player for instant volume adjustment and mute control without leaving your video.

---

### 🔒 100% Privacy & Local Processing
Audio Master operates entirely on your local machine using Chrome's native Web Audio API. No audio data, browsing history, or personal identifiers are ever recorded, collected, or uploaded to any server.

---

### 💡 Support & Compatibility
Tested and optimized for modern web streaming platforms including YouTube, Spotify Web Player, Netflix, Twitch, Vimeo, SoundCloud, and educational platforms.
